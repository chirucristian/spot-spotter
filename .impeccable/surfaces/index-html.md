# Surface Brief: index.html

## Scope & Mode
- **Primary Target:** `index.html`
- **Visitor Mode:** Operate
- **Audience & Task:** Smartphone user standing in a public square who wants to pinpoint their location, immediately read the names of surrounding buildings directly over their footprints on the map, and tap any building name to view its photo and architectural summary.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An outdoor surveyor's architectural field plate that labels every civic landmark directly over its building footprint with numbered iron-gall and vermilion callout banners, refusing the generic map-app mystery pin field where building identities hide until clicked.

OWN-WORLD: Sanborn Fire Insurance Atlas & Architectural Cadastral Plate (`#F4EFE3` surveyor buff ground, `#181A18` iron-gall ink, `#D93B2B` vermilion civic masonry, `#D89B1D` ochre stone, `#2358A6` cobalt water, `#2B6E46` park verdigris). Raised by `ikeda-datamatics` (zero-latency binary inversion between high-contrast daylight buff and night-survey mode), `notation-diagram-systems-star-atlas` (monumental magnitude dot scaling so primary landmarks anchor the eye before reading type), `vernacular-ephemera-jet-age-ticket-wallet` (persistent "INSPECTED" stamp tick on buildings already viewed in the square), `japanese-high-density-web` (1px hairline-ruled multi-cell building badges packing index number, category tab, building name, and distance without bloat), and `rw-lexicon-shoulder-and-plate` (quarantining full-color landmark photography inside a double-ruled chromolithograph plate frame with dynamic first-to-last visible monument range in the top header rule).

STORY: The visitor opens Spot Spotter in a square, sees their live GPS beacon (or selects a famous civic plaza preset), immediately identifies which building is the Parliament, Palace of Justice, Cathedral, or National Museum via persistent rooftop name flags, and taps a badge to pull up a crisp architectural dossier with photo, distance, compass bearing, and summary.
