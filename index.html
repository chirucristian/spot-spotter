The meeting place of the Parliament of the United Kingdom (the House of Commons and the House of Lords). Rebuilt in Perpendicular Gothic style by Charles Barry and Augustus Pugin after the fire of 1834, its 300-meter Thames facade and Elizabeth Tower (Big Ben) anchor the eastern flank of Parliament Square.
